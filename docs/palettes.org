* Supported palettes 🎨

A curated collection of gorgeous, modern, and highly accessible colour palettes
seamlessly integrated for both your system and Emacs.

** The Ef Collection

- *Dark:* ~ef-autumn~, ~ef-bio~, ~ef-dream~
- *Light:* ~ef-day~, ~ef-kassio~, ~ef-melissa-light~, ~ef-cyprus~

** Accessible and classic

- ~modus-vivendi~ and ~modus-operandi~, and all their variants:
  ~-tinted~, ~-deuteranopia~, and ~-tritanopia~
- ~solarized-light~ and ~solarized-dark~
- ~kaolin-dark~

** 🐈 Catppuccin

All four flavours, so a palette named in someone's editor is a palette this
machine can wear.

- ~catppuccin-latte~
- ~catppuccin-frappe~
- ~catppuccin-macchiato~
- ~catppuccin-mocha~

** Modern and neon

- ~dracula~
- ~tokyo-night~
- ~heavy-metal~

** 🌲 Earthy and organic

- ~everforest-dark~ and ~everforest-light~
- ~gruvbox-dark~ and ~gruvbox-light~

** Nordic and pastel

- ~kanagawa~
- ~nord~
- ~rose-pine~, ~rose-pine-moon~ and ~rose-pine-dawn~

** The Doom pantheon

- ~doom-one~ and ~doom-one-light~
- ~doom-tomorrow-night~
- ~doom-molokai~ (similar to Monokai)

** Ayu themes

- ~ayu-dark~, ~ayu-mirage~, and ~ayu-light~

** Golden Gate

- ~golden-gate-light~ and ~golden-gate-dark~ use a system palette:
  the window backgrounds, ink, secondary label greys and system blue of that
  system.  Their wallpaper sets are its day and night artwork, and they ask every
  app for a higher transparency than the other palettes so the desktop reads as
  a translucent system surface rather than a flat panel.

** 🪟 Windows XP / Luna

~windows-xp~ is the Luna desktop kept rather than modernised: the title bar's
blue (~#0056d6~) over the window face's beige (~#ece9d8~), the dialog ink of the
period (~#1b1b1b~, 14:1 on that face) for text, and a quieter grey for secondary
labels.  It is a *light* palette, so the bar mixes its material towards the light
neutral, the terminal opens translucent over the beige rather than over a dark
panel, and the dock wears its light look.

- *Semantic colours:* ~background~ ~#ece9d8~, ~background-l~ ~#f7f4ea~,
  ~primary~ ~#0056d6~, ~primary-l~ ~#316ac5~ (Luna's selected-item blue),
  ~text~ ~#1b1b1b~, ~text-l~ ~#4a4a4a~
- *Tela accent:* ~blue~, in the light variants - the same symbol GTK is told, so
  the icons cannot follow a different accent
- *Emacs:* the editor has no theme of its own name for it, so this is one of the
  few entries in the exception table: it loads ~modus-operandi~, the nearest
  light theme with the same black-on-paper ink
- *btop and the dock:* the brightness defaults, because they are already right -
  ~solarized-light~ and ~vanilla-light~
- *Wallpapers:* the green Norwegian hills, as close as the catalogue comes to the
  hill behind every Luna window

#+begin_quote
Changing the palette is a first-class operation. Pick one from the palette
picker (~Mod+Shift+C~, or the bar's palette glyph) or set ~heks-linux-palette~
in your local settings, then run ~maak install-theme~. Every palette-dependent
surface follows, icons included.
#+end_quote
