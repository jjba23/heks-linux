* Supported palettes 🎨

A curated collection of gorgeous, modern, and highly accessible colour palettes
seamlessly integrated for both your system and Emacs.

** The Ef Collection

- *Dark:* ~ef-autumn~, ~ef-bio~, ~ef-dream~
- *Light:* ~ef-day~, ~ef-kassio~, ~ef-melissa-light~, ~ef-cyprus~

** Accessible and classic

- ~modus-vivendi~ and ~modus-operandi~, and all their variants:
  ~-tinted~, ~-deuteranopia~, and ~-tritanopia~
- ~solarized-light~ and ~solarized-dark~
- ~kaolin-dark~

** 🐈 Catppuccin

- ~catppuccin-latte~
- ~catppuccin-mocha~

** Modern and neon

- ~dracula~
- ~tokyo-night~
- ~heavy-metal~

** 🌲 Earthy and organic

- ~everforest-dark~ and ~everforest-light~
- ~gruvbox-dark~ and ~gruvbox-light~

** Nordic and pastel

- ~kanagawa~
- ~nord~
- ~rose-pine-moon~
- ~rose-pine-dawn~

** The Doom pantheon

- ~doom-one~ and ~doom-one-light~
- ~doom-tomorrow-night~
- ~doom-molokai~ (similar to Monokai)

** Ayu themes

- ~ayu-dark~, ~ayu-mirage~, and ~ayu-light~

** Golden Gate

- ~golden-gate-light~ and ~golden-gate-dark~ use a system palette:
  the window backgrounds, ink, secondary label greys and system blue of that
  system.  Their wallpaper sets are its day and night artwork, and they ask every
  app for a higher transparency than the other palettes so the desktop reads as
  a translucent system surface rather than a flat panel.

#+begin_quote
Changing the palette is a first-class operation. Pick one from the Rofi theme
picker (~Mod+Shift+C~) or set ~heks-linux-palette~ in your local settings, then
run ~maak install-theme~. Every palette-dependent surface follows, icons
included.
#+end_quote
